# Code of conduct

Nea is an open project. We want all participants to have a good experience,
whatever their origin, identity or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect, also when you disagree.
- Criticize ideas and code, never the person who wrote them.
- Assume good intentions. Ask questions before you make assumptions.
- Accept calmly when the project does not accept a proposal.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, mockery, harassment or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments of any type.
- Publication of private data of a different person without permission.
- Repetition of a topic after a request to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainer of the project can edit or delete comments, close issues and
pull requests, and block persons who do not obey these rules. To report a
problem privately, use the private GitHub report (*Security* → *Report a
vulnerability*). Tell us that the report is about conduct.
